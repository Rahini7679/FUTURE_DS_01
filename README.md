# FUTURE_DS_01 - Business Sales Performance Analytics

## Project Overview
This project analyzes Superstore sales data to identify business trends, profitable categories, top-performing products, and regional sales performance.

## Dataset Information
- Records: 9,994
- Columns: 21
- Missing Values: 0

## Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Google Colab

## Key Performance Indicators

| Metric | Value |
|----------|----------|
| Total Sales | $2,297,200.86 |
| Total Profit | $286,397.02 |
| Total Orders | 5,009 |
| Total Quantity Sold | 37,873 |

## Visualizations
- Sales by Category
- Profit by Category
- Sales by Region
- Top 10 Products by Sales
- Discount vs Profit
- Monthly Sales Trend

## Key Insights
- Technology generated the highest sales revenue.
- Technology generated the highest profit.
- West region recorded the highest sales.
- Higher discounts often reduced profitability.
- Sales peaked during November and December.

## Business Recommendations
1. Focus marketing on Technology products.
2. Reduce excessive discounting.
3. Invest more in high-performing regions.
4. Improve Furniture profitability.
5. Prepare inventory for peak sales months.

## Conclusion
The analysis demonstrates how data-driven insights can improve business decision-making and profitability.
